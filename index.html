about

Hi, I'm Busolami (boo · so · lah · me)

I recently completed an MPhil in Machine Learning and Machine Intelligence at the University of Cambridge, where I focused on Speech and Language Processing. Before Cambridge, I spent over a year working as a software engineer at an early-stage technology startup.

I’m particularly interested in research engineering: building machine learning systems, understanding how they behave, evaluating them carefully, and turning research ideas into reliable software. My current interests include NLP and LLMs, AI evaluation, trustworthy machine learning, and high-stakes deployment.

I’m also deliberately strengthening my software engineering foundations, particularly around backend systems, testing, debugging, and building software that other people can depend on. I enjoy work that sits somewhere between research and engineering, but I’m equally interested in becoming a stronger software engineer in my own right.

My MPhil dissertation brought a lot of these interests together. I worked with Police Scotland on trustworthy speech recognition for policing, exploring multi-model ASR, LLM-based fusion, meaning-aware evaluation, and confidence estimates for human review.

projects

2026

Trustworthy ASR for High-Stakes Transcription

MPhil dissertation with Police Scotland. Built a four-model ASR ensemble and evaluated LLM-based fusion for high-stakes transcription, where standard Word Error Rate can miss meaning-altering errors. Developed a five-level meaning-alteration metric, validated seven LLM judges against human labels, and added sentence-level confidence to support human review. The best fusion approach improved meaning preservation on operational police audio from 6% to 13%.
